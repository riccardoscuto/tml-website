The Paint Layer

Brush detail straight onto your model — each layer gets its own image (512–4096), full alpha, per-layer blend modes and a live viewport preview.

A Paint layer — a hand-painted image mapped to the model.

What it does

The familiar paintable layer. Each one owns an image texture at the resolution you choose, and you paint on it with Blender's texture-paint tools with live preview.

Because it is a real image bound to the UVs, a paint layer is the right tool for anything that has to sit in an exact spot — logos, decals, hand-placed grime, lettering.
