The Group Layer

Fold related layers into a folder — collapse, reorder, and fade the whole set with one opacity slider. Nest groups for complex stacks.

Related layers folded into a single group.

What it does

A group is a folder for layers, exactly like Photoshop. Collapse it to tidy the stack, drag it to reorder everything inside at once, and give the whole group a single opacity.

Groups nest: a rust group can live inside a metal group, so you can dial entire effects in and out without touching the individual layers.
