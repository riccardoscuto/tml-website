Layer Type · Group

Group Layer

Fold related layers into a collapsible folder, move them as one unit, and fade the whole group with a single opacity slider. The organisational layer for bigger stacks.

A group — several layers folded into one collapsible folder.

What it does

A group is a folder for layers, like a Photoshop group. Collapse it to keep a long stack readable, drag it to move everything inside at once, and give the whole group one opacity that fades the bundle together.

Groups nest, so a complex material stays organised — a “paint” group, a “wear” group, a “detail” group — each opened only while you’re working in it.

Controls

Keep a big stack manageable.

Collapse

Hide a group’s contents to keep the layer list short and scannable.

Move as one

Reorder a whole group in the stack with a single drag.

Group opacity

One slider fades everything inside — the whole effect in or out at once.

Nesting

Groups inside groups, for layered and modular materials.

In practice

A reusable “rust kit”, faded with one slider.

Rust kit
  • Colour the rust tint layer.
  • Roughness rough where the rust sits.
  • Bump the raised crust — all three folded into one group.
Good to know
  • One opacity — dial the whole rust effect 0–100%.
  • Reusable — duplicate the group to repeat the effect elsewhere.
  • Maskable — mask the group to confine the whole effect to one area.
