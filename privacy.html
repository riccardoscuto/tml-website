What this site is

This is a simple showcase website for the Texture Layer Manager add-on for Blender. It is a set of static pages — there are no accounts, no logins, and no forms to fill in.

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking of any kind — no Google Analytics, no advertising pixels, no third-party trackers. Nothing about your visit is recorded or profiled by us.
